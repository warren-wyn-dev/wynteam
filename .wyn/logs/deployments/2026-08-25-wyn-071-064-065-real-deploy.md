**How it was caught**: right after the run-32837045512 "success" report, Founder opened `wynteam.vercel.app` on their phone and got a Vercel `404: NOT_FOUND`. Investigating, the 404 itself turned out to be a *separate* problem layered on top: merging the follow-up docs PR (#181) to `main` triggered Vercel's own GitHub-integration auto-deploy for the `wynteam` project (visible in the PR's bot comments -- 4 projects: `wynteam`, `wynteam-cesp`, `wynteam-ipe1`, `wynteam-z3rr`, all auto-deployed on every push, completely independent of `deploy-web.yml`). Re-running `deploy-web.yml` (run 32841103332) would have just fixed `wynteam.vercel.app` again -- cancelled it instead once Founder's separate message confirmed `web-neon-sigma-66.vercel.app` (still serving fine throughout, HTTP 200, completely unaffected by any of this) was the actual target.
